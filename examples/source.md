# The Bell Keeper

Mira repairs bells in the coastal town of Bellweather. Her brother Ivo keeps the tide records. The townspeople call Mira the Bell Keeper because she tends the warning bell above the harbor.

The Tide Council closes the harbor during storms. Its keeper must ring the warning bell when the outer marker disappears beneath the water. Mira fears sounding a false alarm after an earlier mistake, but Ivo shows her that the marker has vanished.

Mira rings the bell. The Council opens the hill refuge, and the harbor families reach it before the storm. Afterward, Mira and Ivo agree to check the marker together before every watch.
